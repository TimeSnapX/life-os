## Daily cage (workdays)

- **4:00** — up
- **4:00–4:50** — light body + food + leave
- **5:00** — shift starts
- **2:00–5:00** — finish (variable)
- **Home → short money/admin** — today's list only (log hours in Pay Ledger, due bills, one GreenLedger check). Stop when the list is empty — not when the whole debt plan is done.
- **Body next** — stretch / walk / karate if scheduled
- **Career** — only on some days (see weekly)
- **Wind-down → bed 9:00–9:30**

Hard rules:
- Hours given to Chrona are **gate-to-gate** (start→finish); unpaid break default **30 min**
- Protect bedtime on work nights

---

## Weekly cage

- **Mon–Sat** — run the daily cage (short money/admin → body; career light or skip)
- **Optional midweek career pocket** (Tue/Wed) only if finished by ~3pm — otherwise skip
- **Wednesday payday ritual** (after work) — Pay Ledger (slip when it lands), GreenLedger weekly allocations, confirm what leaves the account before weekend OT spend temptation
- **Saturday OT** — money win; keep evening shorter; protect 9–9:30 bed hard
- **Sunday** — main recovery + deep work: GreenLedger / bills calendar, MC career study, meal/life reset, optional longer body

Career lives mostly on **Sunday + one optional midweek pocket**, not every work night.

---

## Monthly cage

Anchor: **first free Sunday** (deeper review) + **every Wednesday payday** (execute)

First free Sunday review:
1. **Money** — Pay Ledger vs payslips, GreenLedger balances, snowball target for the month
2. **Bills calendar** — Home Ops: rent, phone, insurance, lender dates confirmed
3. **Career checkpoint** — one licence/ticket step or seat-time log toward HC/MC
4. **Body check** — smokes / stretch / karate: what stuck, what to cut
5. **Buffer rule** — if OT spiked, park surplus to buffer/debt before lifestyle
6. **Admin catch-all** — paperwork that didn't fit daily lists

---

## Quarterly cage (~13 weeks)

- **Debt scoreboard** — freeze status, what's cleared, what's next
- **Career quarter** — licence/ticket milestone (HR tenure clock, HC booking, courses budget)
- **Body quarter** — habits that survived six-day weeks; cut what didn't
- **Money quarter** — buffer months saved, OT average, take-home vs plan
- **Life ops** — rego / insurance / medical / DG path dates on the calendar

---

## Yearly cage (Sept → Sept)

Aligns with the MC three-year map.

- One page: income, debt delta, licence progress, health, "still aimed at the Kenworth?"
- Reset Pulse habits and yearly goals
- Book next year's big ticket (course, medical, deposit target)
